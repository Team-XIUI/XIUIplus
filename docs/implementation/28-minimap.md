## Current behavior

Open **Minimap** in configuration, or use `/xiui minimap`. The module has its own enable switch, position, width, shared contextual visibility, profiles and settings search. New installations place it near the upper right. Disabling it removes the edit preview as well as the live display.

- **Shape:** Square, Circle or Wide (16:9). Height follows width; horizontal and vertical map scales remain equal. Circle clips the texture to a 96-segment disc with a matching rim. Square/Wide use the native window frame or a matching rounded rim when corner rounding is enabled.
- **Map artwork:** Regular or Enhanced (Remapster). Includes 431 Regular maps and 231 Enhanced maps. Enhanced falls back to the same native floor's Regular art where unavailable. Unknown maps/floors show an unavailable state.
- **Zoom:** 1–8, remembered per zone. North-up orientation, with a directional player arrow at the center. Scroll over the visible map shape to adjust by 0.25 per wheel notch; partial wheel deltas retain their proportion. The shared settings slider remains the keyboard/controller route. Mouse input outside the map, over its caption or beyond Circle/rounded corners passes through normally.
- **Coordinate edges:** General has one Coordinate edges setting with Left, Top, Right and Bottom ON/OFF buttons in a row, all on by default. Each enabled edge lifts the original 16/512-wide coordinate band from the current Regular/Enhanced artwork and pins it inside the frame. Letters/numbers track the visible grid and grow with zoom. Source bands are cropped from the scrolling art to prevent duplicates; disabled edges retain their natural artwork behavior. Strips respect Circle/rounded clipping and map limits, draw over markers, and share map opacity. North and the default zoom readout leave room for enabled edges. Preferences persist through the shared settings/profile paths; the display adds no input capture.

The map bundle retains the artwork's alpha. Opaque Regular sources recover transparency only in dark areas connected to the outside of the coordinate band, preserving detached coordinate glyphs. The scrolling map, backing, cone and markers are clipped out beneath enabled strips, so burned cutouts reveal the game directly.

The coordinate-edge change passed `xiui_config_input_smoke --minimap-only` and standalone startup. The single DLL was installed in the active Phoenix development setup; AgentLink confirmed runtime **Oct 17 2026 23:45:16**, GPU text and visible HUD. Saved layout positions were retained. Cody retains visual/gameplay acceptance.
- **Appearance:** width, map/frame/background opacity and separate frame/background enable switches. Square/Wide offer **Edge style → Straight / Rounded / Burnt**. Rounded exposes **Corner radius**. Burnt uses the current artwork's alpha silhouette for the map and backing, with a scorched rim and transparent cutouts. It replaces the native frame and coordinate strips while retaining their saved preferences, and its outline stays fixed as the map zooms. Markers and wheel hit testing follow the silhouette. **Drop shadow** supports opacity, softness and paired X/Y offsets for every style. Existing Rounded corners preferences migrate to Rounded; positions and other preferences persist through profiles/reloads.
- **Vision cone:** optional fading facing indicator, off by default. Minimap → Markers offers its angle, size and opacity. It follows the player arrow and stays inside every map shape.
- **Markers:** independently show nearby NPCs as green dots (on by default), party, alliance, pet and current target, with size/opacity controls. Only validated local entities on the selected map/floor are included. Target uses the shared entity color resolver and an additional ring.
- **Text:** optional north indicator and independent zone name, Coordinates and Map number toggles. Coordinates appear in parentheses, such as `(G-8)`; turn off **Text → Labels → Map number** to remove `Map 1` while retaining the position. Older hidden-coordinate preferences initially keep both labels hidden. The shared **Labels / Zoom** selector exposes independent profiles. Zoom has its own visibility, alignment, X/Y offsets (±600 pixels), font, size, colors and effects, anchored by default inside the bottom-right corner and inset for Circle/rounded shapes. Profiles preserve gradients/effects and participate in global typography; initial Zoom styling inherits the previous label appearance. Offsets move the readout relative to its map anchor and persist under `text.minimap.zoom`.

## Map identity and alignment

[minimap_source](../../src/runtime/modules/minimap/minimap_source.cpp) locates the native map table and terrain floor resolver through unique, checked signatures. The floor call's destination and calling convention were inspected against an existing unpacked AgentLink client dump; no live gameplay call was exercised during this task. The native table supplies the zone, floor ID, display number, signed scale and offsets. Normalized coordinates use the client's 512-pixel map space, retaining continuous positions instead of rounding to native marker pixels.

Native records are filtered once per zone and the selected projection is cached between floor checks. Multimap floor lookup runs at most every 100 ms; single-map zones skip terrain calls entirely. Player position and direction update each visible frame. Other markers refresh every 150 ms; hidden modules skip source updates and clear stale identity before returning. NPC scans consider loaded, visible NPCs within 120 yalms, retain at most 128 dots, exclude hidden actors/players/hostile monsters/trusts and stop when NPC markers are disabled. Single-map zones reuse the known map without a terrain trace per marker. Party indices must match the member server ID. Nonsequential floor IDs, including Lower Delkfutt's Tower's floor 15, select their own art. A multimap zone with unresolved floor identity never defaults to its first image; single-map zones need no floor lookup. Missing/unsupported signatures leave the map unavailable. No map-ownership patch or open native map menu is required.

The Phoenix wiki provides the Regular catalog and CDN exports. Enhanced uses the same pinned Remapster pack's `minimaps/ffxidb` images, whose filenames identify native floors directly. Wiki page numbering is presentation order and cannot safely identify dungeon floors. The image archive retains explicit IDs; no ordinal zip between the catalogs is used.

## Loading and lifecycle

[minimap_maps](../../src/runtime/modules/minimap/minimap_maps.cpp) lazily reads the embedded archive index on first use. One worker decodes the requested WebP image through statically linked libwebp, with Windows Imaging Component retained for legacy images; the render thread uploads only that image. The GPU cache holds at most eight textures (at most 8 MiB). Artwork selection, including missing floors and Regular fallback, is cached until the zone, floor or style changes; device generations still govern texture lifetime. Rounded art uses a texture mesh with a one-pixel alpha fringe; its UVs retain the normal map projection. Dots share fixed-size geometry batches through the design system, retaining their colors, black edging, antialiasing and draw order around the target ring. Circle directions are cached instead of recalculated per dot; zero-opacity marker batches are skipped. Frame/shadow drawing reuses the shared rounded contour. Shadows render behind the map and do not enlarge its editable bounds. New requests replace pending work; device generations discard stale completions. Zoning, logout, unavailable identity and disabling the module clear source state, preventing stale artwork or markers from being shown as a new zone.

The archive is **40.43 MiB**, retaining all 662 entries with images resized to at most 512 square and WebP-encoded at quality 90 from the original downloaded sources. Decoding requires no additional Windows codec. It is embedded in the single DLL and requires no runtime downloads or companion minimap plugin. No atlas decoding or texture creation occurs during initialization. [The generator](../../scripts/build-minimap-maps.py) uses the wiki catalog and a pinned Remapster commit; its download cache is under `out/minimap-downloads`. Notices and the full Remapster CC BY-SA 4.0 license are embedded and seeded beside XIUI's other notices.

Mouse, keyboard and controller use existing shared controls for shape, artwork, zoom, independent Zoom text visibility/position/style and presentation, plus the existing map movement/resize paths.
