XIUI Satchel storage-slip catalog

The 28 storage-slip item rosters in storage_slips.hpp preserve the mapping used
by XIUI Lua modules/satchel/slipslogic.lua. XIUI credits the Ashita Find addon
by MalRD as the roster's source.

Find authors: MalRD, zombie343, sippius (Ashita v4 port).
Source: https://github.com/Sippius/Ashita-v4-addons/tree/main/find
The upstream Find code is MIT licensed; its notice is in LICENSE.txt.

The native decoder is a new bounded implementation. It reads the slip's first
24 extra-data bytes and exposes stored item records for browsing only.
