Native imports fixed literal durations, per-effect overrides and spell
application/display metadata. Secondary effects inferred from damage are
marked unconfirmed until explicit evidence arrives. TP formulas, full
gear/merit/job-point duration models and server overlays are not imported.
Database values remain estimates and clear on observed removal or estimated expiry.
