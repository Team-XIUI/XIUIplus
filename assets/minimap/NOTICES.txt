Enhanced artwork uses the project's minimaps/ffxidb exports, whose filenames
match native zone and floor IDs. Wiki page numbers do not identify native floors.
Both sets are resized to at most 512 x 512 and encoded as JPEG (quality 90,
full chroma resolution) for this distribution. These modified Enhanced images
remain available under CC BY-SA 4.0, with no additional restrictions.
scripts/build-minimap-maps.py regenerates the bundled image archive; its index
contains each zone, native floor, artwork set, image offset and byte length.
The archive's image records can be extracted without decoding or modification.

This plugin does not include or depend on the Ashita Minimap or Windower FFXIDB
plugins. Native client map metadata supplies placement and floor selection.
