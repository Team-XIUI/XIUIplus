The pinned pets.lua supplies jug move names and automaton frame/skill requirements.
blood_pacts.lua is an excerpt of scripts/skills-data.lua (sets.retail[13]) at the
same commit and supplies Summoner Blood Pact levels.
scripts/build-chains-data.py produces src/runtime/resources/chains_data.hpp.
Native Chains uses XIUI's existing Retail/Horizon/Phoenix skillchain data and
shared observation/prediction tracker; see assets/skillchains/NOTICES.txt.
